## TikTok / Reels script idea
**You need your own TikTok account, and I can't record real footage of the app — this needs an actual screen recording from you or someone using it.** Script for a 15-20s clip once you have footage:

- **Hook (0-3s):** Text overlay: "POV: no more arguing about fouls at the club"
- **0-3s to 10s:** Screen recording of the scoreboard catching a foul automatically (the app flags it, shows the point adjustment) — this is the actual "wow" moment, keep it front and center, no narration needed
- **10-15s:** Quick cut to the live scores / rankings screen — "also tracks the whole tour"
- **Caption:** "Built this because pen-and-paper scoring was ruining my Tuesday nights 🎱 free on Android/iOS, link in bio"
- **Sound:** use whatever trending audio fits at the time you post — don't pre-pick one, trending sounds change fast and using a stale one hurts reach
